I'm a 3D Modeling Researcher working at BayOne Solutions on behalf of Google. Roughly speaking, I use techniques from [geometric processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometry_processing) on 3D scans to assess and inform the design of earbuds and wearable technologies. I've also worked as a data scientist, a union organizer, and a financial risk analyst. You can read more about me and my past academic/work experience [here](about_me). 

Although I haven't yet published any blogs, I will be participating in [Genuary 2026](https://genuary.art/), albeit in a limited capacity. I'm hoping to write some dev blogs explaining my inspiration and thought process for these pieces [here](Blog) (though I make no guarantees that these explainers will be released in a timely manner!).
